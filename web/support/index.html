Support

We read everything. A real person answers.

Talk to us

Email support@yappy.gg and say what happened. Screenshots help; so does the version number from Settings → About in the app (there is a “Copy build details” button that grabs everything we need).

Quick answers

  • Locked out? There is no password reset yet — we say so at sign-up, and we mean it. Use a password manager. If the account is genuinely stranded, email us and we will see what can be done.
  • Notifications not arriving? Check the app has notification permission in iOS Settings, and that quiet hours in Settings → Notifications is not doing exactly what you asked it to.
  • Want your data gone? Delete your account in Settings → Delete account. It takes effect immediately and everything is purged for good after 30 days — signing back in during that window cancels it.
  • Found a security problem? Email the address above with “security” in the subject and we will treat it with the urgency it deserves. Please do not post it publicly first.

Report someone

The fastest route is inside the app: long-press the message and choose Report. That freezes a copy for our moderators even if the sender deletes it, which an email cannot do.
