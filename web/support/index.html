A little help, right here.

Can’t sign in?

Choose “Forgot password?” on the sign-in screen. If you can’t access your email or still need help, select Account help above.

Appealing a suspension?

Use “Appeal suspension” in your suspension notice to link the case automatically. You can also choose Appeal a suspension above and include your username. Submitting an appeal doesn’t restore access automatically.

Want to report a message or person?

Long-press the message in yappy and choose Report. This sends the relevant context to our moderators.

Found a security issue?

Email support@yappy.gg with “Security report” in the subject. Please avoid sharing it publicly before we’ve investigated.
