The group chat is the product.

Every other app treats a group as a thread with more people in it. yappy treats it as a place — one you can see into before you walk in, that keeps what you collected together, and that looks like yours.

Read the source Self-host it

A group is a place, not a thread.

Open one and it answers the questions a place answers: who is here right now, what is happening, and what have we gathered between us. Groups are the only raised cards on the home screen — people are quiet rows underneath. The hierarchy is the argument.

Owners get a real say in how theirs looks: an accent, a gradient, a glow, an emoji. Flair colours the ring and the title wherever the group appears, so a customised group reads as somewhere specific rather than a row in someone else's app.

Grow into a space when you outgrow one room.

Any group can become a space — several channels under one membership. It keeps its id, its members, its roles and its invite links, and the history moves into the first channel. Nothing to re-create, no one to re-invite.

Built for groups that actually run something.

🏛️

Spaces & channels

Membership and roles live on the space; channels hold messages. Promote someone once and it applies everywhere, because the channel never had its own copy.

🎭

Named roles

Colour-coded roles with their own permissions — alongside the owner/admin/moderator ladder, not instead of it. Roles grant capabilities; the ladder grants authority over people.

✅

Badges & affiliations

Verified, partner and staff marks, plus Twitter-style affiliations: a badged group can vouch for a member, and its logo sits beside their name. Both sides can withdraw.

🤖

Bots & embeds

A real bot API with token auth, Discord-shaped rich embeds, and automatic link unfurling. Only bots may post rich cards — a hand-crafted card is a phishing kit.

🖼️

Media that stays yours

Uploads go straight to object storage, presigned. Attachments live in a private bucket behind a per-viewer authorisation check — membership decides, not a guessable URL.

🎨

Flair

Accents, gradients, glow and shimmer, set by the owner and rendered wherever the group appears. Flair colours around an element, never restyles it.

No Redis. On purpose.

Postgres carries the event bus (LISTEN/NOTIFY), the job queue (pg-boss), presence and rate limiting. One database to run, back up and reason about. The trade-offs and the swap path are written down rather than discovered later.
