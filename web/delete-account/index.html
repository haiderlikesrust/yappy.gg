Delete your yappy account

This page is for the yappy app (package gg.yappy.app, published by yappy). Deleting your account is built into the app itself — no email, no form, no waiting on support.

How to delete your account

  1. Open yappy and tap your profile picture (top right of the home screen) to open Settings.
  2. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  3. Confirm. That's it — the deletion starts immediately.

Can't sign in? Email legal@yappy.gg from the address on your account and we will run the same deletion for you.

What happens immediately

  • Your email address, username and phone number are removed from the account and freed for reuse.
  • Your profile — display name, picture, banner, bio, status — is wiped and shows only as "Deleted account".
  • You are signed out everywhere, and no one can sign into the account again.

What is permanently deleted within 30 days

  • Your devices and push tokens, security keys, and notification history.
  • Your contacts, follows and followers.
  • Your reactions, and all photos, videos and voice notes you uploaded.

The 30-day window exists so a deletion made in anger — or by someone else with your unlocked phone — is not irreversible for a few weeks. Contact us within 30 days to restore; after that, nothing is left to restore.

What is kept, and why

  • Messages you sent in shared conversations remain, permanently detached from your identity and shown as from "Deleted account". A conversation belongs to everyone in it, and deleting your account does not delete other people's copy of what was said — the same rule every messenger follows.
  • Records we are required to keep for safety and legal reasons (for example, moderation reports that concern the account) are retained only as long as the law or an active investigation requires.

Deleting some data without deleting your account

Inside the app you can delete individual messages you sent (for everyone in the conversation), remove photos and media you shared, and clear your profile fields — all without deleting the account.
