Download for Android
  • Download the APK on your phone.
  • Open it. Android will ask whether to allow installs from your browser — this is normal for an app that is not from the Play Store.
  • Sign in, or make an account. Nothing else to configure.

Verify what you installed. Every release is signed with the same key, and an APK signed with any other one is not ours however it reached you. Check with apksigner verify --print-certs — the SHA-256 should be:

844ed3a0 6d6f9d8a 2fe2ffaf 0805594c
8a546ecd db2d7d7f 3c0071dc 36d030f3
